The best portrait wasn't planned.

We were taking a break between setups when the subject looked toward the window. The light was perfect, so I grabbed the camera.

That became the frame I kept coming back to.

<AiSummary>
A portrait session that shifted from planned poses to spontaneous moments, using soft window light and natural expressions.
</AiSummary>

## The setup

One camera, one lens, and a large window providing most of the light.

## The approach

Instead of directing every movement, I let the subject move naturally and looked for expressions that felt genuine.

## The final frame

The strongest photograph came between setups rather than during the planned shots.
