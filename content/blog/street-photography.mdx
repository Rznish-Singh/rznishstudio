Rain changes the city.

The roads get quieter, shop lights start reflecting on the pavement, and people move a little differently.

I went out with one camera and no particular shot in mind. Most of these frames came from simply waiting for something to happen.

<AiSummary>
A monsoon street photography walk through Dehradun focused on reflections, movement, and spontaneous everyday moments.
</AiSummary>

## The walk

No planned locations. Just walking, observing, and waiting for interesting interactions between people, light, and the environment.

## The light

The best frames came just after sunset when the remaining daylight mixed with shop lights and reflections on the wet roads.

## The frames

Nothing was staged. The goal was to document the city as it naturally looked that evening.
